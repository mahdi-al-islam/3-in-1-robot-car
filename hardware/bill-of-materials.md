# Bill of Materials

## Hardware Components

- Microcontroller: Arduino Uno R3
- Motor Driver: L293D Motor Driver IC / Shield
- Bluetooth Module: HC-06 Bluetooth Module
- Ultrasonic Sensor: HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor
- Servo Motor: SG90 Micro Servo Motor
- Chassis: Custom PVC Board Chassis (12" x 18")
- Motors: TT Gear Motors with Wheels
- Power Source: 2x 18650 Li-ion Batteries with Battery Holder
- Wiring: Jumper Wires
- Tools: Soldering Kit
